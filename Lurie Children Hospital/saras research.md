# Saras Research: Lurie Children's Hospital

## Overview

Lurie Children's Hospital is for children only, located in Chicago. It is **#1 in Illinois** and ranked in all **11 children's specialties** by U.S. News.

---

## Biggest Standout

Newsweek ranked Lurie **#1 in the U.S. for heart care and lung care in 2026**. Heart care has held that spot **three years in a row**.

---

## Shared Health Records

Lurie lets other children's doctors use its computer record system. That way, a child's health history is all in one place, and every doctor can see it.

---

## Research Partnership

Lurie's research team works closely with **Northwestern University**, and the two are just one block apart. Sometimes they study a problem together with other hospitals and share what they learn.

---

## Privacy and Security

Lurie follows a law that protects health information, but mistakes have still happened:

- **2019:** A worker looked at records they had no reason to see.
- **2024:** Lurie shut down its computers after seeing suspicious activity.
